# The best TV series

A short list of TV series worth watching:

- **Father Brown** — British detective series about a crime-solving Catholic priest in the 1950s.
  https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2215842/

- **Sister Boniface Mysteries** — Spin-off from Father Brown, following a nun with a talent for forensic science.
  https://www.imdb.com/es-es/title/tt15407098/

Both are light, clever mystery shows perfect for relaxing.
