## 🔍 Detective & Mystery

### Father Brown
**British detective series about a crime-solving Catholic priest in the 1950s.**

- 🎭 Clever, gentle, and full of moral puzzles
- 🇬🇧 Set in the English countryside
- 📚 **IMDB:** [Father Brown](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2215842/)

---

### Sister Boniface Mysteries
**Spin-off from Father Brown, following a nun with a talent for forensic science.**

- 🔬 Light, smart, and fun
- 🧪 Forensic science meets convent life
- 📚 **IMDB:** [Sister Boniface Mysteries](https://www.imdb.com/es-es/title/tt15407098/)

---

### Detective Murdoch
**Canadian mystery series set in late 19th-century Toronto.**

- 🕵️ Follows Detective William Murdoch as he solves crimes
- 🔬 Uses innovative forensic techniques ahead of his time
- 📚 **IMDB:** [Detective Murdoch](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1091909/)

---

## Key Insight

> **The best detectives come in unexpected forms** — a priest, a nun, and a Victorian-era investigator. Wisdom and wit solve more than brute force.
