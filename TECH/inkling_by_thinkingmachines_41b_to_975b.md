# Inkling by Thinking Machines: 41B → 975B Parameters

## The Discovery

**Thinking Machines** released a new open-weight LLM called **Inkling**.

What's remarkable: it starts at **41B parameters** and can be expanded through training all the way up to **975B parameters**.

## Key Facts

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Name** | Inkling |
| **Creator** | Thinking Machines |
| **Starting Size** | 41B parameters |
| **Maximum Size** | 975B parameters (via training) |
| **Type** | Open-weight AI model |
| **Model Card** | [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/Inkling) |
| **IBM News** | [IBM Think News](https://www.ibm.com/think/news/thinking-machines-inkling-adds-name-open-weight-ai) |

## Why This Is Interesting

- 📈 **Growable architecture:** Most models are released at fixed sizes. Inkling seems designed to scale up as you train it.
- 🧠 **41B is already usable** for many tasks, while 975B enters the territory of the largest closed models.
- 🔓 **Open weights** mean researchers and developers can inspect, modify, and deploy it independently.
- 🤝 **IBM partnership/coverage** suggests this is being positioned as a serious alternative in the open model space.

## What to Watch

- How does training scale? Is it efficient?
- What datasets and methods are needed to grow from 41B to 975B?
- How does performance compare to Llama, Kimi, Qwen, or DeepSeek at similar sizes?
- Licensing terms for commercial use
