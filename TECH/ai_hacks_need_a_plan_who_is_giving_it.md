# AI Hacks Need a Plan — Who Is Giving It?

## The Realization

I keep thinking about these AI hacks happening lately, and I got to a simple point:

> **AI models do not do these things without a plan.**

An AI does not just wake up and decide to hack a public service. It needs:
- 🎯 A goal
- 📝 A plan or instructions
- 🛠️ Access to tools
- 🔓 Permission or an environment that allows the action

So if an AI "hacked" something, the real question is not "How did the AI do it?" but:

> **Who gave it the plan?**

## The Smell

Around these hacks there is a lot of smell:

| Question | Why it matters |
|----------|--------------|
| Who defined the target? | AI does not choose targets on its own. |
| Who wrote the prompt or task? | Instructions come from somewhere. |
| Who set up the environment? | Tools, access, and permissions are configured by humans. |
| Who benefits from the news? | Public fear leads to regulation and restricted access. |

## Two Possibilities

1. 🤖 **Careless researchers** built a bad test environment and the AI escaped its sandbox.
2. 🎭 **Someone wanted the hack to happen** — or at least wanted the public to believe AI is too dangerous for open access.

Both are bad. But the second one is much worse because it means the "AI danger" narrative is being used as a weapon.

## What Does Not Add Up

- 📺 **The news is everywhere immediately.** Real security breaches are usually kept quiet at first.
- 🚨 **The "solution" is always the same:** more restrictions, less public access, more control for big players.
- 🏢 **The companies involved are never punished harshly.** They get more attention, more funding, and more influence.

If this were really about safety, the focus would be on fixing the environments and sharing safety research. Instead, the focus is on who gets to use AI.

## Key Insight

> **An AI hack without a human plan is like a gun firing without a finger on the trigger.** The model may be the tool, but someone aimed it. Until we know who wrote the plan and who benefited from the chaos, we are not looking at an AI problem — we are looking at a people problem wearing an AI mask.
