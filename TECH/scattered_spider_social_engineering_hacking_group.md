# Scattered Spider: Social Engineering Hacking Group

## What It Is

**Scattered Spider** is a modern cybercriminal group known for combining **social engineering**, **physical hacking**, and **technical exploitation** to breach major organizations.

Rather than relying purely on malware or zero-days, they often:

- Call help desks pretending to be employees
- Use stolen or synthesized identity information
- Trick IT staff into resetting passwords or issuing MFA bypass codes
- Physically access devices or facilities when digital routes fail

## Why It Feels Familiar

> *"They hack like I was thinking when I was 20 years old."*

The approach is intuitive: **humans are often the weakest link**. Before fancy exploits, before AI-powered attacks, the simplest path is usually:

1. Find a person with access
2. Convince them you're supposed to have access
3. Walk through the door they open for you

Scattered Spider turned that instinct into an organized, global operation.

## Key Tactics

| Tactic | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Social Engineering** | Phone calls, fake identities, pretexting |
| **SIM Swapping** | Hijacking phone numbers to intercept MFA codes |
| **Help Desk Fraud** | Convincing support staff to reset credentials |
| **Physical Access** | On-site intrusion when digital routes fail |
| **Affiliate Model** | Working with ransomware groups (like ALPHV/BlackCat) |

## Why It Matters

- 🌍 **Global reach:** Members spread across multiple countries
- 🏢 **High-value targets:** Telecoms, casinos, technology companies, healthcare
- 🧠 **Low-tech, high-impact:** Doesn't require nation-state tools — just patience, research, and manipulation
- ⚠️ **Hard to defend:** You can't patch a human with a software update

## Defense Takeaways

- **Verify identity aggressively** — especially for password resets and MFA changes
- **Limit help desk authority** — no single person should be able to fully compromise an account
- **Train employees** — social engineering awareness is as important as antivirus
- **Segment networks** — so one breached account can't move everywhere
- **Monitor for unusual access** — especially after credential resets
